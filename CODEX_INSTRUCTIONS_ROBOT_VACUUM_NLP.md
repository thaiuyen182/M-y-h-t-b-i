## Stage A — Data audit and cleaning
- Load `comments` / `text` with pandas; retain `source_row_id` (Excel row number) and original `text_raw`. Identify and remove null/empty, exact duplicate and optionally near-duplicate comments; separately count each action.
- Normalize Unicode to NFC, HTML entities, whitespace, repeated punctuation, zero-width characters, URLs and markup. Remove or mask phone numbers, email addresses, and social handles in exported example comments; never expose personally identifying contact details. Keep Vietnamese diacritics, negation (e.g. `không`, `chưa`, `chẳng`), brand/model names, product codes, and diagnostic terms (e.g. `lỗi`, `pin`, `cảm biến`). Avoid destructive blanket regex or deleting emoji before sentiment inference; retain a minimally normalized `text_for_sentiment` and a separately prepared `text_for_topic`.
- Detect likely promotional/sales/service solicitations using auditable multi-signal rules (contact details + `inbox`/`ib`/`zalo`/`nhận sửa`/`báo giá`/`chốt đơn`/discount calls to action, etc.). IMPORTANT: a message that describes a real issue AND offers a service may contain valuable insight. Mark it `mixed_commercial`, preserve issue content where possible, and analyze sensitivity both including and excluding mixed messages. Do not classify as spam solely because a brand, price, warranty, or repair term appears.
- Output `commercial_label` in {organic, commercial, mixed_commercial, uncertain}, `commercial_reason`, `is_duplicate`, `cleaning_status`; produce `cleaning_audit.csv` and a small reproducible stratified review sample `commercial_review_sample.csv` (masked). Default primary analysis includes organic + relevant mixed, excludes commercial and duplicates; publish sensitivity results for organic-only.
- Vietnamese word segmentation/stopwords may be applied for keyword extraction or topic representation, but DO NOT remove stopwords from raw sentences used for sentence embeddings or sentiment inference. Avoid eliminating negation. Ensure very short but meaningful comments (e.g. `Lỗi cảm biến rồi`) are retained. Make minimum text length configurable.

## Stage B — Vietnamese sentiment

Verify its actual labels, domain, and inference behavior; do not assume class index ordering. If no credible Vietnamese model can be downloaded, stop with a clear error rather than fabricating scores. Do not use VADER/TextBlob as the primary Vietnamese classifier.
- Batch inference on `text_for_sentiment` with deterministic settings. Produce `sentiment_label` (positive / neutral / negative), `sentiment_score` (signed score in [-1,1], defined explicitly from class probabilities, e.g. P(positive)-P(negative)), `sentiment_confidence`, and individual class probabilities when supported. If the model has no neutral class, document this and apply a justified calibrated or explicitly heuristic neutral threshold; distinguish heuristic from validated accuracy.
- Caveat: `lỗi cảm biến` can be a neutral diagnosis rather than negative emotion. Manually inspect a stratified sample of ~50–100 comments (or as many as available), particularly negation, sarcasm, diagnostics, mixed commercial content, and model uncertainty. Export review sheet with predicted labels for human checking; do not claim manual validation occurred unless a human actually labeled the samples.
- Save `sentiment_distribution.csv` and `sentiment_distribution.png` (counts and percentages for positive, neutral, negative), plus `sentiment_score_distribution.png` (histogram). Charts: clean white background, legible Vietnamese fonts, clear title/axis labels, n annotated, 160+ dpi.

## Stage C — BERTopic
- Use `BERTopic` with multilingual/Vietnamese-capable sentence embeddings (prefer a documented multilingual SentenceTransformer), UMAP, HDBSCAN, and a Vietnamese-aware CountVectorizer/representation strategy. Do not feed stripped stopword-only keyword strings into sentence embeddings. Use seeded UMAP (`random_state=42`), document stochastic components and CPU performance.
- Detect corpus size and adapt `min_topic_size`, `min_cluster_size`, `n_neighbors`, and vectorizer `min_df` sensibly. If data are too sparse, explicitly report limits and avoid pretending to have robust clusters. Keep outliers as topic -1 / `Other / Unassigned` in the audit; do not silently omit them from denominator.
- Assign every retained comment `topic_id`, `topic_probability` if available, and human-readable `topic_name_vi`. Generate names from representative comments and keywords, NOT just machine-generated generic labels. Names should be concise and descriptive; mark any labels requiring human review. Do not invent a topic that is not evidenced by comments.
- Save `topic_summary.csv` (topic id, Vietnamese name, count, share of ALL retained comments, top keywords, representative masked examples), `comment_level_results.csv`, `topic_sentiment_crosstab.csv` (topic x sentiment, count and percentage), and `topic_bar_chart.png` (horizontal bars, descending topic sizes, Vietnamese names, counts + percentages; show outlier separately and explicitly). - Also create `topic_sentiment_heatmap.png` to show negative share by topic and optionally a brand/topic mention breakdown (Roborock, Ecovacs, Dreame etc.) when the brand is explicitly present; do not infer brand from unspecified comments.
